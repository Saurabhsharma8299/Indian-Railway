About Indian Railways

Indian Railways is one of the world's largest railway networks comprising 115,000 km of track over a route of 68,000 km and 7,300 stations. Operating in 29 states and 7 union territories, it runs more than 13,000 passenger trains daily.

Our Achievements

  • World's 4th largest railway network
  • Carries over 8 billion passengers annually
  • Transports 1.2 billion tonnes of freight yearly
  • Employs over 1.3 million people
  • 100% electrification target by 2030
